Frequently Asked Questions

Adults should have a comprehensive eye exam every 1-2 years, depending on age, risk factors, and whether you currently wear corrective lenses. If you are over 60, have diabetes, or have a family history of eye disease, annual checkups are strongly recommended. Children should have their first eye exam at 6 months, again at age 3, and before starting school.
An optometrist is a primary eye care provider who performs eye exams, vision tests, prescribes corrective lenses, and detects eye abnormalities. An ophthalmologist is a medical doctor who specializes in eye and vision care, including performing eye surgeries and treating complex eye conditions. Both play important roles in maintaining your eye health.
Many vision insurance plans cover routine eye exams, and some medical insurance plans cover exams related to eye diseases or conditions. Coverage varies widely by provider and plan. We recommend contacting your insurance company directly or speaking with our billing department to understand your specific benefits before your appointment.
Common signs that you may need glasses include frequent squinting, headaches or eye strain, difficulty reading small print, blurry vision at a distance, double vision, and increased sensitivity to light. If you notice any of these symptoms, schedule an eye exam so we can evaluate your vision and recommend the best solution.
LASIK (Laser-Assisted In Situ Keratomileusis) uses a specialized laser to reshape the cornea, correcting refractive errors like nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism. The procedure typically takes about 15 minutes per eye, has a high success rate, and most patients experience significantly improved vision within 24 hours. A consultation is required to determine if you are a suitable candidate.
Yes, children as young as 8-10 years old can wear contact lenses, provided they are mature enough to handle the responsibility of proper lens care and hygiene. Contact lenses can be an excellent option for children who are active in sports or feel self-conscious wearing glasses. A comprehensive eye exam and fitting with an eye care professional is essential before making the switch.
